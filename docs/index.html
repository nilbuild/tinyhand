Everything a pointer should do on camera

TinyHand lives in your menu bar. Turn it on, point at what matters, and turn it off when you're done.

Tap

Taps, not clicks

Clicks become a soft thock and a little press. Nothing underneath gets clicked, focused or dragged.

Draw

Draw on anything

Hold and drag to circle a number or underline a line of text. Ink fades after a few seconds, so the screen stays clean.

Pointers

Pick one from a small toolbar at the bottom of the screen. It fades away while you record and comes back when you need it.

Questions

Why does TinyHand need Accessibility?

To turn your clicks into taps. macOS only lets an app catch clicks before they reach other apps with your permission. TinyHand walks you through it on first launch and never clicks anything on its own.

Does it work with my screen recorder?

Yes. TinyHand draws on top of your screen, so QuickTime, Screen Studio, Loom, OBS and the built-in screenshot tool all record it.

Can I still use my Mac while it's on?

Menus, the menu bar and TinyHand's toolbar work as usual. Everywhere else, clicks are taps until you press Esc or ⌃⌥H.

How does it update?

TinyHand checks for new versions and updates itself. You can turn that off in Settings.
